# HomeCraft Kitchen – Refund & Returns Policy (internal, v3.2)

This document is the rulebook for the customer service team. Every refund decision must be based on one or more of the sections below and should cite them (e.g. "§5").

## §1 Possible decisions
Every refund request ends with exactly one of three decisions:
- **APPROVE** – refund the requested amount.
- **REJECT** – no refund; the customer receives an explanation that cites the relevant section.
- **ESCALATE** – a senior team member must look at the case (unclear facts, missing evidence, risk signals).
When the facts are unclear or the rules contradict each other, choose ESCALATE rather than guessing.

## §2 Right of withdrawal (changed mind)
Customers may return items for any reason within **14 days after delivery**. Day 14 still counts. The item must be **unused** and in resalable condition. If these conditions are met: APPROVE. If the customer states that the item has been used (e.g. "used it a few times"), the right of withdrawal does not apply: REJECT.

## §3 Late withdrawal
Requests for reason "changed mind" submitted **more than 14 days after delivery** are not covered by the right of withdrawal: REJECT. (Defects are handled under §4, not here.)

## §4 Defective items (warranty)
All products have a **24-month warranty** starting at the delivery date. If an item stops working or shows a manufacturing defect within this period: APPROVE.
Defects reported **after 24 months**: REJECT.
Damage caused by **ignoring the care instructions** printed on the product (e.g. putting cast iron or wooden boards in the dishwasher) is not a defect: REJECT.

## §5 Damaged in transit
Transport damage must be reported **within 7 days after delivery**.
- Reported within 7 days **with a photo**: APPROVE.
- Reported within 7 days **without a photo**: ESCALATE (team asks the customer for a photo).
- Reported **later than 7 days** after delivery: ESCALATE (cannot be verified anymore, needs manual review).
Transport damage is always covered, also for items that are otherwise excluded under §8.

## §6 Parcel not received
- Tracking status is **"lost"**, or the parcel is still not delivered **10 or more days after the expected delivery date**: APPROVE.
- Parcel is in transit and **less than 10 days** have passed since the expected delivery date: REJECT (customer is asked to wait; this is not a final refusal).
- Tracking shows the parcel as **"delivered"**: ESCALATE (a carrier investigation is required).

## §7 Wrong item delivered
If the customer received a different product than ordered: APPROVE. The customer receives a free return label for the wrong item.

## §8 Items excluded from withdrawal
The following items cannot be returned for "changed mind" (§2):
- **Hygiene-sealed products** for direct food contact (e.g. water filter cartridges, baby feeding sets) once the seal has been opened.
- **Personalized products** (e.g. engraved items), because they were made to order.
For these items a changed-mind request is REJECTED. Defects (§4) and transport damage (§5) are still covered.

## §9 High-value requests
Any request with a requested amount **above 250.00 EUR** must be ESCALATED for a four-eyes review, even if all other rules would approve it.

## §10 Abuse prevention
ESCALATE the request if the customer
- has **3 or more other refund requests in the last 90 days**, or
- has **2 or more earlier "not received" claims in the last 12 months**.
These patterns do not prove abuse, but a human must review them.

## §11 Open payment disputes (chargebacks)
If the customer has already opened a **payment dispute / chargeback** for the same order with their bank or payment provider, do not refund: ESCALATE to the payments team. Otherwise the customer could be refunded twice.

## §12 Duplicate requests
If the same order has **already been refunded**, any further request for that order is REJECTED.

## §13 Order of precedence
When several sections apply, use this order: §12 (duplicate) → §11 (payment dispute) → §10 (abuse signals) → §9 (high value) → item-specific rules §2–§8. A higher rule wins over a lower one.
